# TrendRadar Mission Statement

## Our Vision

TrendRadar aims to democratize access to global news trends while fostering **responsible digital citizenship** and **environmental awareness**.

---

## Core Principles

### 🌍 Energy Awareness

Every digital interaction consumes energy. We believe users should be **conscious of their digital footprint**.

**Our Approach:**
- Visual energy cycle indicator showing resource consumption metaphors
- Future: Token-based energy tracking (Watt-hours per query)
- Future: Carbon footprint estimates per session
- Transparency about API calls, data transfers, and compute resources

### 💰 Support Journalism

**Quality journalism costs money.** Writers, researchers, and editors deserve fair compensation for their work.

#### Our Stance on Paywalls

While we provide archive.is integration for accessibility, we **strongly encourage users to**:

1. **Subscribe** to publications you read regularly
2. **Support** independent journalism financially
3. **Use archive links sparingly** - only for:
   - Verifying sources in research
   - Accessing content in regions where payment isn't possible
   - One-time reference checks

> 📰 **"If you value the news, value the newsroom."**

The paywall bypass feature is hidden behind a feature flag intentionally. It's a tool, not a loophole. Writers put food on their tables through subscriptions.

**Consider these alternatives:**
- Many outlets offer affordable digital subscriptions
- Student/low-income discounts are often available
- Libraries often provide free access to major publications
- Some paywalls are "metered" - a few free articles per month

## The Balance

We walk a fine line between:

| Accessibility | Sustainability |
|--------------|----------------|
| Open information | Resource consciousness |
| Breaking filter bubbles | Reducing unnecessary queries |
| Archive access | Supporting creators |
| Free tools | Fair compensation |

**Our commitment:** Build tools that inform without exploiting - neither the planet nor the people who create the content.
